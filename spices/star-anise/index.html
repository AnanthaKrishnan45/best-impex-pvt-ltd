SPICE KNOWLEDGE

Traditional Uses & Health Research

Characteristic compounds
Trans-anethole and shikimic acid
Traditional context
Star anise is used in traditional aromatic broths and infusions.
Scientific research
Shikimic acid has been used as an industrial starting material in pharmaceutical synthesis; the spice itself is not an antiviral medicine.
Safety and precautions
Use only verified edible Chinese star anise; Japanese star anise is toxic. Avoid giving star anise teas to infants.
