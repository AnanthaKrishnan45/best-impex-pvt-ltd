SPICE KNOWLEDGE

Traditional Uses & Health Research

Characteristic compounds
1,8-cineole and alpha-terpinyl acetate
Traditional context
Cardamom is traditionally used in aromatic after-meal preparations.
Scientific research
Small human studies have examined inflammatory and metabolic markers; clinical conclusions remain limited.
Medicinal research context
Human trials have explored cardamom and inflammatory biomarkers. Results are preliminary and do not establish a medical indication. Research reference.
Safety and precautions
Do not substitute concentrated cardamom oil for the culinary spice.
